Why Budapest

A world-class production hub — at a fraction of Western European rates.

Budapest has been one of Europe's most active film production cities for decades — Hollywood blockbusters, streaming originals, and global brand campaigns are produced here regularly. The city has a deep talent pool of cinematographers, gaffers, colourists, and production coordinators.

For international brands, this means: the same cinema-grade production quality you'd expect from London, Paris, or Amsterdam — at production costs that are typically 30–50% lower. Vision Studio brings that advantage with a fully English-speaking workflow.
